## Task 1: Understand the Business Problem

* The Problem to Investigate: Investigating operational friction, financial leakage due to zero-payer coverage encounters, high-frequency procedural costs, and patient readmission patterns within historical healthcare datasets.  
* Why It Matters to Healthcare Operations: Unmanaged encounter costs, uninsured patient loads, and high readmission rates strain hospital resources, impact financial sustainability, and degrade patient care quality.  
* Intended User of Deliverable: Hospital administrators, clinical operations managers, and financial analysts.  
* Decision Supported: Resource allocation, contract negotiations with insurance payers, workflow optimization for high-volume procedures, and targeting chronic care management to lower 30-day readmissions.  
* Limitations of Evidence: Historical data lacks real-time operational feeds, qualitative clinician notes on patient severity, and external out-of-network care records.
